1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files downloaded to your device when you visit certain web pages, which allow information about your browsing to be stored and retrieved. This policy explains which cookies Nudade uses and for what purpose.

2. Cookies we use

This site is hosted on Netlify and uses the technical cookies strictly necessary for it to work (for example, those related to the secure submission of forms). It also stores your cookie choice in your browser (key nd_consent_v1, first party, 12 months). These do not require consent.

Only if you give your consent, the following third-party cookies are activated:

  • _fbp · Provider: Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd. · Third party · Purpose: measure the effectiveness of our ads on Facebook and Instagram and show them to people more likely to be interested · Duration: 90 days.
  • _fbc · Provider: Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd. · Third party · Purpose: remember that you arrived from a Meta ad, to attribute the visit correctly · Duration: 90 days.

With your consent, the Meta Pixel records page visits and specific actions such as submitting the valuation, calculator or call-back forms and clicking on WhatsApp. We do not send Meta the content of the forms (name, phone or email). Meta may also process this data for its own purposes, under its privacy policy, and transfer it to the US under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

We do not use third-party analytics cookies such as Google Analytics.

3. Types of cookies by purpose

Cookies can be classified as technical or necessary, preference, analytics or measurement, and advertising cookies. Technical cookies are essential for the site to work and are enabled by default. The Meta advertising cookies described above are only installed with your prior, explicit consent.

4. Managing and disabling cookies

You can allow, block or delete the cookies installed on your device by adjusting your browser settings. You will find instructions in the help section of Chrome, Firefox, Safari or Edge. Please note that disabling technical cookies may affect how the site works.

5. Consent

On your first visit you will see a notice with two options of equal weight: "Accept" and "Reject". Until you accept, no advertising cookie is installed. If you reject, the site works exactly the same.

You can withdraw or change your consent at any time from the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of the page; doing so deletes Meta cookies from this domain. We will ask you again after 12 months.

6. Updates to this policy

This policy may be updated in line with regulatory changes, the development of the project or the cookies used. We recommend that you review it periodically.
