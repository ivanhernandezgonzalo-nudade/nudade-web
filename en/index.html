What bare ownership is

A legal arrangement with over 200 years of history.

In Spain it’s called nuda propiedad. Three simple steps, no surprises. The price reflects age and life expectancy: the older the owner, the higher the amount. A transparent formula, certified by a notary.

Get an estimate
STEP 01

The bare ownership is sold

The owner sells the title to the home while keeping the right to live in it for life. The agreed amount is paid into their account.

STEP 02

They keep the right to live there for life

They carry on living at home exactly as before. Nobody can come in, change anything or ask them to leave.

STEP 03

The buyer receives full ownership

When the lifetime right ends, the buyer receives full ownership of the home, free of encumbrances.

Real guarantees

Peace of mind isn’t promised. It’s signed before a notary.

Official independent valuation

We work with valuation firms approved by the Bank of Spain. The value is set by an independent third party, not by us.

A notary of your choice

Your parents choose a notary they trust. We adapt to them, not the other way round.

Registered lifetime right

The right to live in the home is recorded in the Spanish Land Registry. Nobody can revoke it. Not even us.

Legal guidance for the family

We also support children and heirs throughout the process. No pressure, no artificial deadlines.

Paid in full at completion

The amount is transferred on the day of signing. No deferred payments, no hidden conditions.

Complete confidentiality

The process is discreet. Nobody around them will know anything unless the family chooses to share it.
