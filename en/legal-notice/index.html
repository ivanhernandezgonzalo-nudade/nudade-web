This legal notice governs access to, browsing and use of the website. The information provided, including the calculator results, is for guidance only and does not replace the official valuation or the professional advice that a real transaction would require, which, where applicable, would be handled through the relevant qualified or licensed professionals (notary, approved valuer, legal and tax advisers).
