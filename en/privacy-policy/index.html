In short

Nudade processes the data you provide through its forms, by phone or via WhatsApp in order to deal with your request, give you an indicative valuation and, if you wish, support you through the transaction. You can exercise your rights by writing to info@nudade.es.

2. Data we collect

Through the site's forms (valuation request, calculator, call-back request and investor sign-up) and through subsequent contact by email, phone or WhatsApp, we may collect: name, email address, phone number, preferred contact time, city or province of the home, age and number of owners, relationship with the owner, indicative value of the home, preferred payment option, area of investment interest and the content of the messages you send us.

If you decide to proceed with a transaction, we may also process the data needed to complete it (identification and property details). We do not collect special categories of data or payment details through the site.

3. Purpose of processing

We process your data in order to: deal with your request for information or a call-back; prepare an indicative valuation of the bare ownership; manage, if you so decide, the brokerage mandate and the sale and purchase of the bare ownership; send you, if you have signed up as an investor, information about available homes that match your interest; and comply with applicable legal obligations.

No automated decisions with legal effects are made based solely on this data.

4. Legal basis

Your consent, given by ticking the corresponding box and submitting the form (Article 6.1.a GDPR); the taking of pre-contractual steps and the performance of the mandate or contract when you decide to proceed with a transaction (Article 6.1.b GDPR); and compliance with legal obligations, such as tax or anti-money-laundering obligations (Article 6.1.c GDPR).

5. Retention period

Data from requests that do not lead to a transaction is kept for a maximum of 12 months from the last contact, unless you ask for it to be deleted earlier. If a contractual relationship is established, data will be kept for as long as it lasts and, afterwards, for the periods required by applicable law. Once those periods have elapsed, it will be irreversibly deleted or anonymised.

6. Recipients and data processors

Your data is hosted and managed through technology providers acting as data processors: Netlify (web hosting and form handling), Google (email) and WhatsApp (messaging, when you contact us through that channel). Some of them may process data outside the European Economic Area, relying on the appropriate safeguards provided for in the GDPR (standard contractual clauses or adequacy decisions).

When you proceed with a transaction, and only to the extent necessary, your data may be disclosed to the other party to the sale, to partner real estate agencies involved in it, to the notary and to the professionals taking part in its completion. Outside these cases, data will not be disclosed to third parties unless required by law.

7. Your rights

You may at any time exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and portability, and withdraw the consent you have given at any time, by writing to info@nudade.es and stating the right you wish to exercise. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es) if you consider that the processing does not comply with the regulations.

8. Security

Appropriate technical and organisational measures are applied to protect your data against loss, misuse or unauthorised access, taking into account the nature of the data processed and the associated risks.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect regulatory changes or changes in Nudade's services. Any material change will be published on this page with the date of revision.
